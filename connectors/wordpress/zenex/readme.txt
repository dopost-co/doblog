Publishes the articles you write in Zenex on your WordPress site.

== Description ==

Write and plan articles in Zenex; this plugin keeps a copy of each published article on your site as a regular WordPress post:

* Images are copied into your media library, and the cover becomes the featured image.
* Categories and tags are created as needed.
* SEO title, description, canonical URL and social fields go to Yoast SEO, Rank Math or SEOPress when one is active, or are printed by the plugin otherwise.
* With Polylang, each language is published in its language and translations are linked.
* Unpublishing or deleting an article in Zenex moves its post to the trash.

Your site never depends on Zenex to show articles: they are stored in your WordPress database.

== Installation ==

1. Upload the plugin (Plugins → Add New → Upload Plugin) and activate it.
2. In Zenex, open your blog → Settings → Web integration and copy the connection key.
3. Paste it in Settings → Zenex and save.
4. Back in Zenex, click “Check connection”.
