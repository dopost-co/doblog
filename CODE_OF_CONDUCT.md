# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in Zenex a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive and specific.
- Assume good intent while taking responsibility for impact.
- Welcome questions and different levels of experience.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on ideas and evidence.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, sustained disruption and personal attacks are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior is harmful or unsafe. Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options at [dopost.co](https://dopost.co). Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and avoid conflicts of interest when assigning a reviewer.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing the project.
